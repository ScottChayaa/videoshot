videoshot

Turn moments from YouTube videos into your own photo library.

What it does

  • Pick frames from any YouTube video. Paste a link, browse every available frame of the video, and keep the ones you like.
  • Browse by time, find by tag and place. Your saved frames form a photo library grouped by month, with tags, places and albums.
  • Turn your own photos and videos into a video. Arrange photos and clips from your phone on a simple timeline, trim them, and upload the result to your own YouTube channel as an unlisted video. Then pick frames from it like any other video.
  • Back up to your own Google Drive. Your library is stored on your phone; backups go to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so you can restore on a new phone.

Your data stays with you

videoshot has no server and no account system. Your library lives on your phone and in your own Google Drive. The app uploads videos to YouTube only when you tap Export, and only to the channel of the Google account you choose.
