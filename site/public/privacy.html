Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

videoshot is a personal Android app. It has no server: the developer does not receive, collect, or store any of your data. Everything described below happens between your phone and Google services, under your own Google account.

Data on your phone

  • Your library (saved frames, tags, places, albums, descriptions) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Photos and videos on your phone. When you create a video in the app, it reads only the photos and videos you select. If you choose to remove them after a successful upload, the app deletes exactly those files, after Android asks you to confirm (or, if you have granted the app "Media management" access in Android settings, without a separate confirmation).

Google user data the app accesses

The app asks for Google permissions only when you use a feature that needs them, and only for these purposes:

PermissionWhat the app does with it
Basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) Shows which Google account is connected on the Account screen.
Google Drive — app data folder
drive.appdata		 Saves and restores backups of your library in a hidden folder that only videoshot can see. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
YouTube — upload videos
youtube.upload		 Uploads a video you created in the app to your own YouTube channel as an unlisted video, only when you tap Export. The app does not read, change or delete your other videos, and does not post anything else.

Access tokens stay on your phone and are used only to make these requests directly to Google. The app does not send your Google data to the developer or to any third party, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

videoshot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

videoshot uses YouTube API Services. By uploading videos through the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

To browse a video's frames, the app also loads publicly available video information and preview images from YouTube, the same way a web browser does. No account information is sent with these requests.

Sharing

The app does not share your data with anyone. Videos you upload appear on YouTube under your own account with the visibility you chose (unlisted by default).

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnect your Google account on the app's Account screen, or revoke access at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes your library from your phone.
  • Backups in Google Drive can be deleted in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → videoshot → Delete hidden app data.
  • Videos you uploaded can be deleted in YouTube Studio.

Children

videoshot is not directed to children under 13.
